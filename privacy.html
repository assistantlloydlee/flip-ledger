Flip Ledger - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Flip Ledger is a browser extension for Chrome that shows a watch dealer the real margin on a watch listing page they are viewing and, with a licence, comparable prices for the reference.

What the extension handles

  • The listing page you are viewing. To show a figure in the page, the extension reads the price and the title from the listing page already open in your browser. That reading happens on your own machine, and the figure is shown back to you in the page.
  • Your own data. The watches, sales, rates and observations you enter are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. They stay on your computer and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your licence key. If you buy the paid unlock, the extension sends the licence key to Gumroad, and only to Gumroad, to check that the key is valid. That is the only thing the extension ever sends off your machine, and it is sent only when the key is entered or re-checked.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect your browsing history.
  • It does not read your cookies, your passwords or your accounts.
  • It does not track you, and it does not show advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It makes no network request for the free features. The free margin tool works with the network switched off.

Where your data lives

All of your own data is in your browser's local extension storage. It leaves your machine only if you copy it out yourself, or clear it, which you can do at any time from the extension's options page ("clear all data").

The licence check

The paid unlock is verified against Gumroad's licence API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) using your licence key. The result is cached locally for a set period so the extension does not need to re-check on every use. Only the key is sent.

Use of data received from Google APIs

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
