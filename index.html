What it does

Open an eBay listing or a Chrono24 offer. Flip Ledger reads the price on screen, applies your own fee stack, and shows what the watch would actually leave you with, in the page, while you are deciding.

The free tier (complete, permanently)

  • Landed cost, not the sticker price. Hammer or asking price, the auction buyer's premium through your own tier table (bands behave like tax brackets, with an open ended top band), shipping and insurance, import duty, service or repair, parts.
  • The fee stack for eBay, Chrono24 and dealer to dealer, with a typed override for when your invoice disagrees with the default.
  • Net proceeds and margin in the page, before you commit, not after.
  • Consignment kept separate. The dealer's share and the consignor's amount are two numbers, never blended into one.
  • Your own rates. Every rate and every tier is yours to set once, on the options page.

None of that is limited by time, count or an account. It is the whole margin tool, free.

The paid part ($9 once): the comps view

The paid part is not a bigger version of the free tier. It is a different kind of information: what comparable pieces actually sold for. Every figure carries its sample size, its range and its date span, so you can see how thin or how solid it is.

  • Comps, supplied for you. Comparable sold prices for the reference. As of 21 September 2026 that is more than 1,200 sold reference level aggregates over 1,900 sold observations, across 21 brands, each shown as a median with the sample count, the range and the date span behind it.
  • Honest by construction. When we hold nothing for a reference, it says so plainly. Nothing is estimated. Asking prices are labelled as asking and are never dressed up as sold prices.
  • One payment, no subscription. The key works on your machine.

What it does not do

It does not buy, bid, list or sell anything for you. It does not touch your accounts, your cookies or your passwords. It reads the price on the listing page you are viewing.

Privacy, plainly

Your watches, sales, rates and observations stay on your computer. The free tier makes no network call at all. When you use the comps view, the extension reads our comps file from our own host, and it checks your licence key with Gumroad. Those two requests are the only network calls in the product. The licence request sends your key and nothing else. No browsing history is collected, no account is needed, and nothing about you is sold. Full privacy policy.
