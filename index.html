Flip Ledger

Real margin on a watch listing page: landed cost, the full fee stack, net proceeds.

Flip Ledger is a Chrome extension for watch dealers. It reads the price on the listing page you already have open, applies the fee stack a dealer actually pays, and shows the real margin. With a licence it adds comparable sold prices for the reference, shown as a median with the sample count and the date span behind it.
